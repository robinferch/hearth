Hearth is a native player for audiobookshelf, the self-hosted server for audiobooks and podcasts. Connect your server and your whole library is on your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and in your car, with your progress in sync everywhere.

Hearth doesn't include any audiobooks or podcasts. You need access to an audiobookshelf server, either your own or one someone shares with you.

YOUR LIBRARY, THE WAY YOU ORGANIZE IT
• Browse audiobooks, podcasts, series, authors, narrators, collections and playlists
• Continue Listening and personalized shelves from your server
• Search all your libraries at once, across several servers
• Filter and sort by progress, genre, tag, release date or length
• Read PDFs attached to a book

A PLAYER MADE FOR LONG LISTENING
• Chapters with progress per chapter, on screen and on the Lock Screen
• Bookmarks with notes, synced with your server
• Playback speed from 0.25× to 4× with presets, saved per book or series
• Smart rewind after a pause, so you never lose the thread
• Configurable skip intervals and chapter jumps
• A queue plus Up Next: the next book in a series or the next episode starts by itself

FALL ASLEEP WITHOUT LOSING YOUR PLACE
• Sleep timer by minutes or at the end of the chapter
• Gentle fade-out, shake to keep listening
• Countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

LISTEN ANYWHERE, EVEN OFFLINE
• Download books and episodes in the background
• Automatic downloads for series and podcasts
• Offline progress and listening time sync back when you're online again
• Live progress updates from your other devices

APPLE WATCH, CARPLAY, WIDGETS AND SIRI
• Apple Watch app: listen on your wrist with Bluetooth headphones, download to the watch, or control your iPhone
• CarPlay with Listen Now, your libraries and the chapter list
• Widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy
• Controls for Control Center
• Siri and Shortcuts: resume, pause, skip, set a sleep timer, play a book or podcast
• Spotlight search and Handoff between your devices

DESIGNED FOR IPAD
• Sidebar with every server and library
• Two-column player with chapters and queue side by side
• Multiple windows and keyboard shortcuts

LISTENING STATS
• Daily listening goal and streak
• Charts for the last week and month, and your most-listened titles

YOUR SERVER, YOUR DATA
• Sign in with username and password or single sign-on (OpenID)
• Several servers and accounts at the same time
• Custom HTTP headers, self-signed certificates and client certificates
• No analytics, no tracking, no account with us
• Open source on GitHub

Hearth is an independent project and is not affiliated with audiobookshelf. audiobookshelf 2.26 or later is recommended.

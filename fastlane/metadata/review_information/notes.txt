Hearth is a client for audiobookshelf, a self-hosted audiobook and podcast server. The app does not include or sell any content; people connect it to their own server.

To review the app, use our demo server:
1. Launch the app and tap "Add Server".
2. Enter the server address shown at the end of these notes (DEMO SERVER) and tap "Verify".
3. Sign in with the user name and password from the sign-in information.

The demo library contains public-domain audiobooks and podcasts. Background audio is used for audiobook and podcast playback. Widgets, Siri shortcuts and the Apple Watch app use the same account.
